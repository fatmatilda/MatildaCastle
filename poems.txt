in matilda’s castle, tall and grand,
she rules the halls with iron hand.
if you seek to flee tonight,
beware her gaze, her endless sight.

the queen of stone, the queen of might,
matilda holds the castle tight.
each door she locks, each key she hides,
so none escape her royal tides.

her crown is sharp, her smile is cold,
a thousand secrets she has told.
to leave her keep you must be sly,
or in her dungeon you will lie.

matilda’s throne is carved from fear,
she whispers threats for all to hear.
the walls have ears, the floors have eyes,
so run, but she will hear your cries.

the castle gates are forged with care,
matilda’s magic lingers there.
to slip away you’ll need a plan,
for she’s the queen since time began.

her servants lurk in every hall,
they answer matilda’s every call.
one wrong step, one sound too loud,
and you’ll be wrapped in her dark shroud.

the queen of shadows, queen of night,
she dims the torches, kills the light.
but find a crack, a hidden stone,
and you might flee her castle throne.

matilda counts the silver spoons,
she knows the phase of every moon.
she’ll notice if a mouse has fled,
so watch your step, or lose your head.

her portrait follows as you pass,
a regal sneer behind the glass.
she sees your fear, she knows your name,
to leave her castle is no game.

the drawbridge rises at her call,
a heavy gate, a solid wall.
but find a tunnel, dig a hole,
and escape her castle’s iron soul.

matilda’s puzzles, locks, and keys,
she hides her secrets in the trees.
solve her riddle, break her chain,
or in her keep you will remain.

matilda’s garden’s full of thorns,
a maze of rose and twisted horns.
the path is false, the gate is sealed,
her royal power is revealed.

she keeps a raven on her arm,
a bird that warns of any harm.
so if you run, the raven flies,
and matilda’s wrath will soon arise.

the castle clock ticks slow and deep,
while matilda watches in her sleep.
one eye is closed, one eye is wide,
she’ll know the moment you decide.

her armour shines, her sword is bright,
she’ll hunt you through the endless night.
but every queen has just one flaw,
so find her weakness, break the law.

the dungeons echo with her name,
a whispered threat, a candle flame.
to escape her castle’s cold design,
you’ll need a plan and steady spine.

matilda’s feast is grand and cold,
she’ll toast your health, then take a hold.
the goblets clink, the shadows fall,
she’ll have you trapped within her hall.

a tapestry of woven gold,
shows matilda’s legends, brave and bold.
but pull the thread, unveil the door,
and flee her castle evermore.

the queen of matilda’s castle keep,
has secrets buried dark and deep.
she’ll chase you down the winding stair,
so move with speed and breathe the air.

to leave her castle is a test,
of mind and nerve and iron vest.
but matilda’s reign will end one day,
so run, and find your escape way.
